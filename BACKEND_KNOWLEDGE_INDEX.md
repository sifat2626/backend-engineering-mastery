# Backend Knowledge Index

Cross-phase living reference indexing the concepts and technologies studied across the program.

> **Entry Rule:** Add or update entries only as concepts are actively studied and verified in the current phase. Do not pre-populate future phases.

## Required Knowledge Depth Levels

Our approved learning framework defines **exactly three official knowledge-depth levels**. Mechanism-level depth and production-level understanding are dimensions of learning applied across these levels, not additional official knowledge levels. Do not force every technology to Practical Competence.

| Level | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Awareness` | Know what a concept or technology is, what problem it solves, its role in the ecosystem, and when it may or may not be appropriate. |
| `Working Understanding` | Understand its key mechanisms, invariants, alternatives, trade-offs, and failure modes well enough to reason about designs, debug problems, and make informed engineering decisions. |
| `Practical Competence` | Be able to design, implement, verify, and operate it effectively in practice, especially within our primary Node.js and PostgreSQL specialization. |

## Ecosystem Coverage Areas (Target Breadth)

Concepts and technologies from the following 12 areas in `MASTER_ROADMAP.md` will be indexed here as they are taught in each phase (explaining relationships, distinctions, mechanisms, trade-offs, and failure modes—not as a disconnected glossary, and never falsely marked mastered):

- **Architecture:** Monolith, modular monolith, microservices, SOA, event-driven architecture, CQRS, event sourcing.
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, DynamoDB, ACID, MVCC, indexing, replication, sharding.
- **Networking:** TCP, UDP, DNS, TLS, HTTP/2, HTTP/3, WebSocket, gRPC, reverse proxies.
- **Concurrency:** Threads, processes, event loop, mutexes, semaphores, race conditions, deadlocks.
- **Messaging:** Kafka, RabbitMQ, NATS, SQS, Pub/Sub, delivery guarantees, idempotency.
- **Caching:** Redis, CDN, cache-aside, write-through, invalidation, eviction.
- **Security:** OAuth 2.0, OIDC, JWT, RBAC, ABAC, CSRF, XSS, SSRF, SQL injection.
- **Infrastructure:** Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, load balancers.
- **Reliability:** SLI, SLO, SLA, circuit breakers, retries, backpressure, graceful degradation.
- **Observability:** Logs, metrics, traces, OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana.
- **Performance:** Profiling, benchmarking, latency, throughput, connection pooling, query optimization.
- **Cloud:** AWS, GCP, Azure, serverless, containers, object storage, managed databases.
