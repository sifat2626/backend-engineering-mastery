# Learning Philosophy

> **Authority:** Core principles below reflect the approved program philosophy owned by ChatGPT (Curriculum Owner & Instructor).

## Core Engineering & Learning Principles

- **Deep conceptual understanding** rather than superficial familiarity.
- **Mechanism-level explanations** where appropriate (understanding how OS primitives, memory, I/O, runtimes, network protocols, storage engines, and concurrency control actually work).
- **Production-oriented engineering knowledge** grounded in reliability, operability, latency, observability, and maintainability.
- **Explicit alternatives, trade-offs, assumptions, and failure modes** for every meaningful engineering choice.
- **Broad awareness of the backend ecosystem** across languages, databases, queues, caches, protocols, and infrastructure.
- **Practical specialization in Node.js and PostgreSQL** proved through real, reproducible implementations and experiments.
- **Independent reasoning and evidence-based decisions** rather than folklore or unverified assumptions.
- **AI-assisted engineering with verification and accountability:** use AI as a research and engineering partner while independently inspecting, testing, and verifying its output.

## Study Discipline & Focus

- **One active phase at a time:** do not pre-generate or study future phases in parallel.
- **One primary learning topic/resource at a time:** maintain depth and avoid fragmented study.
- **Exposure to a concept does not equal mastery.**
- **Agent-generated content does not equal learner achievement** (and agent time never counts as learner study hours).
- **Mastery must be demonstrated by the learner** through original reasoning, working experiments, and instructor-evaluated checkpoints.

## Knowledge-Depth Framework & Authority

- Use **exactly three official knowledge levels:**
  1. **Awareness**
  2. **Working Understanding**
  3. **Practical Competence**
- **Mechanism-level depth** and **production-level understanding** are dimensions of learning applied across these three levels, not additional knowledge levels.
- **ChatGPT owns phase instruction, assessment, and final approval.**
