## Roles & Authority

- **GitHub Repository:** Single source of truth for curriculum, progress, decisions, notes, and verified labs.
- **ChatGPT (Curriculum Owner & Instructor):** Owns the 29-phase roadmap, phase order, scope, schedule, allocated hours, mastery assessment, and final phase completion approval.
- **Learner:** Responsible for studying, reasoning, experimenting, implementing, and demonstrating mastery.
- **Antigravity (Research & Engineering Partner):** Supports deep conceptual understanding, mechanism-level research, reproducible experiments, and engineering review.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

1. **Curriculum immutability:** Never independently invent, reorder, expand, narrow, or revise `MASTER_ROADMAP.md`, phase scopes, schedules, allocated hours, or progression criteria. Transfer ChatGPT-approved curriculum updates faithfully without altering meaning or structure.
2. **One phase at a time:** Work only on the active phase recorded in `PROGRESS.md` and `NEXT_SESSION.md`. Never pre-generate folders, notes, or materials for future phases.
3. **No fake progress:** Agent-generated notes, scaffolds, or examples never count as completed learning. Only learner-demonstrated work counts as `completed`, and only ChatGPT can mark a phase or milestone `approved`.
4. **Continuity:** Always read `PROGRESS.md` and `NEXT_SESSION.md` before starting work. Never restart an in-progress phase or repeat completed diagnostics/blocks.
5. **Preserve learner ownership:** Do not overwrite learner notes or hand over complete checkpoint solutions. Challenge reasoning, surface edge cases, and review learner work.
